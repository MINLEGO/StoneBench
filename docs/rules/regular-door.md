## Measurement handoff to issue #13

Rules 23868 requires the union of wiring extents over all states and movements, ceiling each axis then multiplying. Endpoint maxima and block count do not satisfy that rule. Rules 24437 and 24616 make exclusions depend on the frame and open hallway pattern; Rules 24688 and 24918 specify entity cases which are outside alpha. Rules 30714 retains the input block as wiring while the input device is excluded. `measurement_roles` provides observed coordinates with their geometric role and conditional exclusion reason; it is not permission to delete arbitrary circuit blocks from a measurement. A moving block retains its role/provenance and intermediate extents. Wiring necessity and the open-state composition exception must be established by the measure/protocol tools before applying a conditional exclusion.

Rules 26513, 26906–27128, 27683 and 28244 establish timing in repeater mode from the input interaction. Retain the signal arrival at the input block separately. Opening ends at the last movement achieving the opened hallway pattern; closing ends at the last door-block movement. Standard extension/retraction ends at movement start +2gt; instant retraction ends at movement start; a 0..2gt pulse extension ends at start + received pulse length. Rest recognition has its own timestamp and does not replace opening/closing completion. This ticket defines those events and exclusions but measures neither dynamically.
